Step Zero

Pack-a-Punch

PaP must be unlocked before you can upgrade any weapon. It requires activating three teleporter pads scattered around the castle. Do this early — ideally before round 10.

Turn on the Power

Follow the power cable symbols on the walls from the starting area. The power switch is in the lower castle. Activating it enables the teleporter pads and opens the gate to the Undercroft.

Activate All Three Teleporter Pads

Find and interact with each of the three glowing teleporter pads around the map — one in the Upper Courtyard, one near the Clock Tower, and one in the Undercroft. Step on each pad and hold the interact button to link them.

Access the Pack-a-Punch

Once all three pads are linked, the Pack-a-Punch machine appears in the Pyramid Room inside the Undercroft. It costs 5,000 points per upgrade. The room also features low-gravity — use the four floor plates around the Pyramid to toggle it.

⬡
Screenshot — Power switch location (lower castle corridor)
The power generator is marked by glowing cable symbols running along the walls from spawn. Follow them down.
You can use the Wunderspheres (glowing launch pads) to travel quickly between areas. They cost nothing and are the fastest way to loop between the courtyard, clock tower, and undercroft.
Prerequisite for All Bows

Dragon Heads

Before the Wrath of the Ancients bow becomes available to purchase off the wall, you must awaken all three dragon heads by feeding them zombie kills. You need roughly 8–9 kills in front of each head. The bow wall buys only appear once all three are satisfied.

This step is easy to miss. If the bow isn't appearing on the wall, you haven't fed all three dragon heads yet.
01
Lower Courtyard Dragon

Located at the far end of the Lower Courtyard, past the Double Tap machine. Face the dragon head and get kills in its eyeline. It will glow and smoke when fully awakened.

02
Control Room Dragon

Found in the Control Room area adjacent to the main courtyard. Get kills directly in front of the carved head — the mouth opens wider with each kill, then clamps shut when satisfied.

03
Undercroft Dragon

In the basement of the Control Room, accessible after the power is on. Feed it the same way — walk past it slowly while getting kills so the kills register within range of its head.

Wonder Weapon

Wrath of the Ancients

The base bow — available off the wall for 500 points once all three dragon heads are fed. It's a solid weapon early on, but falls off past round 20. Upgrade it into one of the four elemental variants as soon as possible. You can only hold one elemental version at a time.

Buy from the Wall (500 Points)

Two wall buy locations: one in the Bastion area near the Upper Courtyard, and one in the lower passage near the Clock Tower stairs. After feeding the dragons, a glowing bow symbol appears on the wall.

Pack-a-Punch It First

Before starting any bow upgrade quest, PaP the Wrath of the Ancients for 5,000 points. The upgraded version (called the "Wrath of the Ancients PaP") deals significantly more damage and is required for some bow upgrade steps to work properly.

Choose Your Upgrade Path

The four elemental upgrades — Wolf, Void, Fire, and Storm — can be completed in any order. Each requires a separate quest chain. You can only carry one upgraded bow, so choose based on your playstyle. Wolf is easiest for beginners; Storm has the highest damage ceiling.

Bow Upgrade 1
Elemental Upgrade · Wolf
Wolf Bow
Kreeholo lu Kreemasaleet
Fires arrows that chain lightning between nearby zombies. Excellent for crowd control and training loops. One of the more beginner-friendly upgrades to complete.
Requires: Wrath of the Ancients (wall buy, 500pts) · All 3 dragon heads fed · Power on

Find and Shoot the Four Wolf Paintings

Locate four wall paintings hidden around the castle. They depict: a king sitting on his throne beside a wolf, a knight on horseback, a castle burning in flames, and a king struck by arrows. Shoot each painting with the Wrath of the Ancients to reveal a glowing wolf symbol beneath it.

Shoot the Red Flag at the Rocket Test Site

Use the Wundersphere or teleporter to reach the Rocket Test Site (the outdoor launch area). Find the red flag on a pole and shoot it with the bow. This causes a wolf skull to drop to the ground.

Place the Skull on the Skeleton

Pick up the wolf skull and take it to the nearby skeleton sitting against a wall in the test site area. Interact with the skeleton to place the skull. This summons a spectral ghost wolf.

Follow the Ghost Wolf and Feed It

The ghost wolf wanders between fixed points around the castle. Follow it and get kills nearby — it feeds on the souls. At each location it stops, shoot the glowing wolf symbol on the wall above the spot where the wolf paws at the ground. You'll need to wallrun in some areas to reach the symbols.

Interact with the Wolf Symbol Box in the Undercroft

Head to the Undercroft and find the glowing wolf symbol carved into the wall near the crafting bench. Interact with it — this begins the final charging phase.

Kill Zombies Until the Box is Fully Charged

Stand near the wolf symbol box and kill zombies. The box glows brighter with each kill. Keep going until it flashes and releases the upgraded bow — pick it up off the ground.

⬡
Screenshot — Ghost wolf paw location with wall symbol above
Each paw spot is marked by a faint glow on the ground. The wolf symbol above it becomes visible only after the wolf has pawed at that location.
The wolf only moves to its next location once you've collected all souls at the current one. Don't rush — kill 8–10 zombies at each paw spot before moving on.
Bow Upgrade 2
Elemental Upgrade · Void
Void Bow
Shadow Bow
Creates a void singularity on impact that pulls nearby zombies in before detonating. Highly effective in tight corridors and chokepoints.
Requires: Wrath of the Ancients · All 3 dragon heads fed · Power on

Locate the Purple Symbols Near the Double Tap Machine

Look up at the wall near the Double Tap Root Beer machine. Purple, glowing symbols are painted on the stonework. Shooting them causes a broken arrow to drop to the floor below. Pick it up.

Fire the Bow at the Gate Trap

Equip the Wrath of the Ancients and shoot an arrow through the Gate Trap while it is active. The arrow passes through the electrified gate and picks up a purple charge. This reveals faint purple painted marks on surfaces around the map.

Find and Shoot the Purple Marks

Track down the glowing purple marks revealed by the charged arrow and shoot each one with the bow to activate them. They appear on walls in the castle interior and courtyard areas.

Go to the Pyramid Symbol Box and Kill 20 Zombies

Head into the Undercroft to the Pyramid room. Find the glowing arrow symbol box on the wall near the pyramid structure. Stand close to it and kill 20 zombies. The box absorbs their souls and glows brighter with each kill.

Collect the Void Bow

After the 20th kill the box flashes and ejects the upgraded Void Bow. Pick it up from the ground near the pyramid.

Bow Upgrade 3
Elemental Upgrade · Fire
Fire Bow
Rune Prison Bow
Detonates in a spreading ring of fire on impact. Exceptional for clearing large groups. Becomes one of the strongest options at mid-to-high rounds when upgraded via Pack-a-Punch.
Requires: Wrath of the Ancients · All 3 dragon heads fed · Power on · Rocket Test Site access

Retrieve the Arrow from the Top of the Clock Tower

Climb or use the Wundersphere to reach the top of the Clock Tower. There is a glowing arrow resting at the highest accessible point. Pick it up — this is the base fire arrow used for the rest of the quest.

Shoot the Magma Rock after the Rocket Test Doors Open

Wait for the rocket test countdown to complete and the large blast doors to open at the Rocket Test Site. Sprint outside and look to the right — on top of a nearby building there's a glowing orange magma rock. Shoot it with the Wrath of the Ancients.

Use the Jump Pad to Shoot Three Red Circles Mid-Air

Use the launch pad (Wundersphere) in the area. While you're travelling through the air, three red glowing circles appear in the sky at fixed points. Aim and shoot each one before you land. You may need multiple attempts to hit all three in a single flight.

Stand in the Circles and Collect Souls

After hitting all three circles, they appear on the ground as burning rings. Stand inside each one and kill zombies — the rings absorb their souls. Keep killing until magma erupts into the sky above, signalling the step is complete. Do this for all three circles.

Collect the Fire Bow

Once the magma bursts from all three circles, the Fire Bow materialises near the centre of the area. Pick it up quickly as the souls finish transferring.

⬡
Screenshot — Mid-air red circles during Wundersphere travel
The circles only appear while you're airborne on the launch pad. Pre-aim before launching to hit all three in one pass.
The red circles move quickly across your field of view during the jump. Launch from the pad, immediately ADS, and sweep left to right. Most players miss at least one on the first attempt — just relaunch and try again.
Bow Upgrade 4
Elemental Upgrade · Storm
Storm Bow
Lightning Bow
Charges then releases a lightning storm that arcs through large groups. Highest single-use damage output of all four bows. Slightly more steps to complete but worth every round it takes.
Requires: Wrath of the Ancients · All 3 dragon heads fed · Power on · Death Ray activated

Activate the Death Ray

The Death Ray is the large electrical weapon mounted in the open courtyard. Interact with the control panel near it to turn it on. It will begin charging — you'll need it active for multiple steps. You can activate it during a round but it takes time to power up.

Shoot Three Bonfires with the Wrath of the Ancients

Three small bonfire stands are positioned around the castle — in the courtyard, near the bastion, and along the keep wall. Shoot each one with the base bow to ignite them. They will glow orange when hit correctly.

Kill Zombies Near the Three Electrified Urns

Find three large stone urns that crackle with electricity — they appear scattered around the courtyard area once the Death Ray is active. Kill zombies near each urn to charge it. The urn pulses brightly as it fills. Repeat for all three.

Charge Arrows Using the Urns, Then Reshoot the Bonfires

Stand next to a fully charged urn and fire a bow shot — the arrow absorbs electricity from the urn as it passes through the field. Immediately shoot the arrow into one of the bonfires you lit in Step 2. Repeat this process for all three bonfires using three separate charged arrows.

Interact with the Electric Fog and Reshoot the Weather Vane

A cloud of electric fog appears near the courtyard after completing Step 4. Interact with the fog — this transforms your arrow into a Reforged Arrow. Then locate the weather vane on one of the castle's stone spires and shoot it with the bow. This causes lightning to strike.

Place the Reforged Arrow on the Lightning Shrine

Find the Lightning Symbol shrine on the ground in the courtyard. Place the Reforged Arrow on it by interacting with the shrine.

Kill 20 Zombies, Then Retrieve the Bow

With the arrow placed, kill 20 zombies near the shrine — their souls charge the lightning strike. After the 20th kill the shrine explodes with light and ejects the Storm Bow. Pick it up.

⬡
Screenshot — Electrified urn glowing when fully charged
A charged urn crackles constantly and emits a visible electric field. Stand close and shoot through it — the arrow will visibly arc blue as it passes through.
⬡

The Storm Bow's charging mechanic takes slightly longer than the other bows to complete. Run the sequence between rounds rather than fighting through it while being pressured by a full horde.
Buildable Wonder Weapon

Ragnarok DG-4

A three-part buildable directed-energy device. When placed on the ground it creates an electromagnetic gravity field that immobilises and kills zombies. Can also be held and fired as a handheld weapon for a close-range gravity slam. Essential for Easter Egg completion and crowd control at high rounds.

Two of the three parts require specific actions to spawn — they won't just be sitting on the map. Read each step carefully before the round starts so you're ready.
01
Part One — Kill the Panzersoldat

The first Panzersoldat spawns automatically around rounds 8–12. When you kill it, it drops this DG-4 component as a loot drop — the same way the Fire Staff piece drops in Origins. Grab it off the ground before the end of the round.

  • Guaranteed drop from the first Panzersoldat kill
  • The Panzersoldat roams the full map — listen for its jetpack sound
  • Specialist weapons and the bow deal extra damage to it
02
Part Two — Wundersphere Mid-Air Grab

After turning on the Death Ray at least once, this part floats above the Bastion wall. It can only be grabbed while in mid-air via a Wundersphere. There are two launch pad locations that can reach it.

  • Wundersphere in the Upper Courtyard — launches toward the Bastion
  • Wundersphere on the second floor of the Lower Courtyard
  • Press Interact while airborne — the part is directly in your flight path
  • Death Ray must be activated first or the part won't be there
03
Part Three — Rocket Launch Site Panel

This part involves interacting with the rocket control panel during the launch countdown. Timing is critical.

  • Use the teleporter near the Pyramid room to reach the Rocket Launch Site
  • Wait roughly 2 minutes for the countdown timer to begin
  • Sprint to the control panel below the Pack-a-Punch location, near the stairs
  • Interact with it — the light changes from red to green
  • Return to the teleporter area and wait for all panel lights to go green
  • Interact with the controls — the part appears inside the teleporter room
⬡
Screenshot — DG-4 placed on the ground, active gravity field visible
The placed DG-4 creates a visible gravitational dome. Zombies walking into it are slowed and crushed. Replace it after each use — it's a single-use deploy per pickup.
Build the DG-4 at any Crafting Station once all three parts are collected. There are crafting benches in the Undercroft and near the Upper Courtyard gate. The DG-4 is also required for a key Easter Egg step, so build it before attempting the main quest.
Buildable — 3 Parts

Rocket Shield

A buildable shield that absorbs damage from behind, reducing incoming hits from regular zombies. Hold it up to block and melee with it for a powerful knockback strike. Each part has three possible spawn locations — only one spawns per game.

Each part has 3 possible spawn points but only one per game will be active. Check all three locations for each part — if it's not at the first spot, move to the next.
01
Part One — Lower Courtyard

All three spawns are within the Lower Courtyard, to the right of spawn past the Double Tap machine.

  • On stacked boxes just inside the gate, immediately to the right
  • On small crates beside the stairs near the destroyed tank in the centre
  • On the ground near the zombie barrier by the far wall
02
Part Two — Clock Tower / Upper Courtyard

Spawns in the Clock Tower interior or the open courtyard outside it.

  • Inside the Clock Tower on or beside the tables near the dead zombie prop
  • On the ground in the main upper courtyard outside the church entrance
  • On the ledge near the clock face, accessible by climbing the interior stairs
03
Part Three — Undercroft

Located in the Undercroft below the church. Requires the power to be on and the low-gravity to be activated.

  • Activate low-gravity: stand on the 4 floor plates around the Pyramid until they glow
  • The part floats high up against the golden wall of the Undercroft
  • Look directly opposite the Crafting Bench, then look up
  • Jump in low-gravity to reach it, or wait for it to drift within reach
⬡
Screenshot — Part 1 spawn on stacked boxes, Lower Courtyard gate entrance

⬡
Screenshot — Part 3 floating against the gold wall in the Undercroft (low-gravity active)

Build the shield at any crafting bench as soon as you have all three parts. Prioritise the shield early — it's cheap to build (just parts, no points) and saves you perks in tight situations. The melee strike staggers any regular zombie in one hit.
